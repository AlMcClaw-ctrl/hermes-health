Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Hermes Health is a private, single-user application. It is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google account data.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads health and fitness data (for example steps, heart rate, sleep and activity) and YouTube data from the owner's Google account.

How data is used and stored

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer for personal dashboards and analysis. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
